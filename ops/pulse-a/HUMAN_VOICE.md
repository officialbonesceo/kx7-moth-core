# Human voice rules for LaneCash writers (pulse-a)

Write like a careful Nigerian friend texting a junior, not like a corporate AI blog.

## Do
- Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences).
- Concrete examples (WhatsApp, OPay, POS, campus).
- Admit uncertainty: “check the official site”, “this changes”.
- Use “you” and plain verbs: check, ask, wait, save, refuse.
- One clear warning box tone without shouting.

## Don’t
- “In today’s digital landscape”, “Moreover”, “It is important to note”.
- Fake statistics or “studies show” without a real source.
- Promise earnings, “guaranteed”, or “easy money”.
- Mention AI, models, or “as an AI”.
- Invent company names, cut-offs, or fees.

## Byline
- Author team: LaneCash Editorial / Scam Desk / Campus Desk only.
- Never invent personal names of “experts”.

## Length
- Aim 500–900 words of real steps, not filler.
